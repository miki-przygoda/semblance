## Why

0.4.0's deliverable is a quotable result: flyvis's model reproduces
flyvis's published claims under this workspace's scoring, the scoring is
provably flyvis's, and both axes can tell a fly-like network from one that
is not. The record is where a reader of 0.5.0's curve checks that. Then the
epic ships.

## Goal

1. A CI job that produces `validate --ensemble --markdown` on hosted CI.
2. `docs/results/0.4.0-fidelity.md`: a hand-written preamble and that
   output, held to the code by `app/tests/record.rs`.
3. `TRAINED_CKA` set from it (task 5).
4. The 0.4.0 bump and tag; publishing waits for the owner.

## Design

### 1. The `fidelity-record` CI job

A new job in `.github/workflows/ci.yml`, on `workflow_dispatch` and on
`v*` tags only (owner's choice), not on every push:

- fetches the fixtures (`ci/fetch-fixtures.sh`, `fixtures-3`);
- builds release, runs `semblance validate --ensemble --markdown --threads
  <runner cores>` with `SEMBLANCE_REQUIRE_FIXTURES=1` and
  `SEMBLANCE_REQUIRE_LIBM_RECORD=1`;
- uploads the output as the `fidelity-record` artifact;
- if a committed record exists, compares its generated block with the
  output byte for byte (outside the libm sections, as 0.3.0) and fails on a
  difference;
- prints its wall time. Its `timeout-minutes` is set from task 3's and
  task 4's measured costs, inside the hosted 6-hour limit; if the measured
  cost does not fit, that goes to the owner before this job is written.

The existing per-push jobs are unchanged apart from gate A's rows (task 3).

### 2. The record

`docs/results/0.4.0-fidelity.md`, as 0.3.0's: a `Generated:` line naming
the CI run, a hand-written preamble, and below the `BEGIN semblance validate
--ensemble --markdown` marker the artifact's output, unedited. It is
committed from the artifact under 0.3.0's rule (`CLAUDE.md`): never
generated by hand or on a workstation.

**The preamble** says what 0.4.0 claims and what it found, each number
appearing in the generated block (`record.rs`'s existing numeral check):

- gate A: which values are exact and which bounded, and the worst ratios;
- gate B: model 000's polarity count and its misses; which ensemble median
  (all 50 or top 10) gave 32 of 32; the DSI classification against the
  paper's; preferred directions within 45°, marked as borrowed from the
  paper's model selection;
- gates C and D: the trained CKA scale; each control's worst draw against
  it, and its redraws; that random rewiring is this project's control, not
  Zhou & Hasler's; our Zhou & Hasler statistics beside theirs;
- the generator finding (task 5);
- task 2's beast-versus-Mac torch outcome;
- anything that did not reproduce, stated as a finding.

### 3. `record.rs`

Extended to the second record, reusing its marker, libm and numeral
machinery:

- **every push:** the rows plain `validate` produces (gate A on 000 and 049
  and its control) are regenerated and must equal their counterparts in the
  0.4.0 record's block; the ensemble rows are not regenerated (too slow) but
  must parse; `TRAINED_CKA`'s four values must each appear in the block;
- **the `fidelity-record` job:** the full block, byte for byte, as above.

Bounded rows whose values depend on the host's libm (the DSI, preferred
direction and tuning reductions) sit inside libm sections, compared only
where the fingerprint matches or `SEMBLANCE_REQUIRE_LIBM_RECORD` is set, as
in 0.3.0.

### 4. `TRAINED_CKA`

Set in `parity` from the committed record's gate D row in the same commit
as the record; `score` then prints its placement instead of "scale not yet
recorded" (task 5).

### 5. The release

- Every crate to `0.4.0`, `Cargo.lock` updated (still 8 packages).
- `CLAUDE.md`: "Current: v0.4.0", the test and skip counts, the record
  paragraph naming both records, the fixture list with `flyvis-indices/`.
- `README.md`: what runs today (`score`, `validate --ensemble`), the crate
  table.
- The epic README: every task marked done, or deferred or removed with its
  reason (the bump rule).
- Tag `v0.4.0`, which also triggers `fidelity-record` once more as a check.
- `cargo publish` of the 0.4.0 crates waits for the owner's go-ahead, as
  every publish does.

## Proof

- The `fidelity-record` job passes on the tag.
- `cargo test --workspace` with fixtures required, clippy `-D warnings`,
  `roundtrip` at `0.000000`, `validate` exit 0.
- `record.rs` fails on a hand-edited number in either record (an existing
  test pattern, extended).

## Out of scope

- Any 0.5.0 work. The curve's y-axis is 0.6.0's choice (decision 7).
